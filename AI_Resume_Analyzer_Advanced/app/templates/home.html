A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clarity starts with good questions.

Here’s what to expect when you use InterviewIQ.

Read the full guide →
What happens when I upload my resume?

InterviewIQ extracts text from your PDF or DOCX, identifies recognized skills, and calculates a resume quality score. Your analysis is available in your account dashboard.

How are job match scores calculated?

Recommendations combine required-skill alignment and text similarity. The default matcher uses TF-IDF; optional semantic matching can be enabled by the site administrator.

Can I improve my match score?

Review the skill gaps shown for a role. If you have that experience, make it easier to find in your resume; otherwise, use the gaps to guide what you learn next.

What file types can I upload?

You can upload PDF or DOCX resumes up to the size limit configured for this site. Your resume analysis and saved matches are connected to your account.
