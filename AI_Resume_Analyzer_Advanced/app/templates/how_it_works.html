HOW MATCHING WORKS

Useful signals, explained simply.

Skills alignment checks the skills detected in your resume against the role’s listed requirements.

Text similarity compares resume and role descriptions. The default matcher uses TF-IDF; optional semantic matching can be enabled by the site administrator.

Missing skills highlights listed requirements that were not detected, so you can decide which ones to build or make clearer in your resume.
